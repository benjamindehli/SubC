SubC is a single triangle wave oscillator from a Dreadbox Erebus v2, sampled note by note and then put through saturation.

The clean samples came through a Roland PA-120 mixer. The saturated samples went on through a Hairball Audio FET/RACK, an 1176-style compressor and a transformer and diode saturator. One knob blends between them, so the instrument travels from a pure sub-bass tone to something with real weight and harmonics on top of it.

A second mode uses stereo versions of the same notes, sent through a TC Electronic SCF Stereo Chorus Flanger in vibrato mode. It is much wider, at the cost of some low end when summed to mono, which is what makes the pair useful: mono for the sub, stereo for the pad.

A Dynamics switch decides whether velocity drives volume and saturation together, and an ADSR envelope shapes the rest.

216 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored as lossless FLAC in one sample file that the installer puts in place.

## Features

- Dreadbox Erebus v2 triangle oscillator, sampled note by note.
- Saturation knob blending clean and driven samples.
- Driven signal through a Hairball Audio FET/RACK (1176-style) and a transformer and diode saturator.
- Mono mode for sub-bass weight; stereo mode through a TC Electronic SCF Stereo Chorus Flanger in vibrato mode.
- Dynamics switch, where velocity drives both volume and saturation.
- ADSR envelope.
- 216 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored losslessly (982 MB source library).
